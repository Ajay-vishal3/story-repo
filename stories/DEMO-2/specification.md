# Specification
## 1. Summary
Implement account-based lockout after consecutive failed login attempts to slow automated password guessing.

## 2. Goals
- Lock accounts temporarily after repeated failed logins.
- Avoid revealing account existence or lockout status through login error messages.
- Allow support staff to unlock accounts manually.

## 3. Functional Requirements
- **FR-1:** After five consecutive failed login attempts on an account, the account must be locked for 15 minutes.
- **FR-2:** A successful login must reset the account’s failed-attempt count to zero.
- **FR-3:** Login attempts must be rejected while the account is locked, including attempts with the correct password.
- **FR-4:** Login error messages must not reveal whether the account exists or is locked.
- **FR-5:** Support staff must be able to unlock an account manually.
- **FR-6:** Lockout must be tracked per account, not per IP address.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria
### FR-1
**Given** an account has fewer than five consecutive failed login attempts  
**When** a login attempt fails and brings the consecutive total to five  
**Then** the account is locked for 15 minutes.

### FR-2
**Given** an account has recorded failed login attempts  
**When** login succeeds  
**Then** its failed-attempt count is reset to zero.

### FR-3
**Given** an account is locked  
**When** a login is attempted with either an incorrect or correct password  
**Then** login is rejected.

### FR-4
**Given** a login attempt is rejected  
**When** an error message is shown  
**Then** it does not reveal whether the account exists or is locked.

### FR-5
**Given** an account is locked  
**When** support staff manually unlock it  
**Then** the account is unlocked.

### FR-6
**Given** failed login attempts originate from one or more IP addresses  
**When** attempts are evaluated for lockout  
**Then** they are counted per account, not per IP address.

## 5. Out of Scope
- Password reset flow.
- Multi-factor authentication.

## 6. Assumptions
- Consecutive failed attempts are tracked per account.
- The 15-minute lockout begins when the fifth consecutive failed attempt occurs.

## 7. Open Questions

- What behavior is expected when the 15-minute lockout period ends?
- What authorization or audit requirements apply to manual account unlocks?
