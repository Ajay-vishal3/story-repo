# Specification

## 1. Summary
Implement account-based lockout after consecutive failed login attempts to slow automated password guessing while allowing successful logins to reset the failure count.

## 2. Goals
- Lock an account for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
- Prevent login while an account is locked, including with a correct password.
- Avoid revealing whether an account exists or is locked in the user-facing error message.
- Allow support staff to unlock accounts manually.

## 3. Functional Requirements
- **FR-1:** After 5 consecutive failed login attempts for an account, the account must be locked for 15 minutes.
- **FR-2:** A successful login must reset the account’s consecutive failed-attempt count to zero.
- **FR-3:** While an account is locked, login must be rejected even when the submitted password is correct.
- **FR-4:** The user-facing error message must not reveal whether the account exists or is locked.
- **FR-5:** Support staff must be able to unlock an account manually.
- **FR-6:** Lockout must be applied per account, not per IP address.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria

### FR-1
**Given** an account has fewer than 5 consecutive failed login attempts  
**When** its fifth consecutive login attempt fails  
**Then** the account is locked for 15 minutes.

### FR-2
**Given** an account has a consecutive failed-attempt count  
**When** a login succeeds  
**Then** the count is reset to zero.

### FR-3
**Given** an account is locked  
**When** a login is attempted with the correct password  
**Then** the login is rejected.

### FR-4
**Given** a login attempt results in an error  
**When** the user is shown the error message  
**Then** the message does not reveal whether the account exists or is locked.

### FR-5
**Given** support staff need to unlock an account  
**When** they manually unlock it  
**Then** the account is unlocked.

### FR-6
**Given** login attempts are made from one or more IP addresses  
**When** failed attempts are counted for lockout  
**Then** the lockout applies per account and not per IP address.

## 5. Out of Scope
- Password reset flow.
- Multi-factor authentication.

## 6. Assumptions
- The lockout duration begins when the account reaches 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
- Lockout tracking is per account, as specified.

## 7. Open Questions
- How should failed attempts made while an account is locked affect the failed-attempt count?
- What exact error message should be shown for login failures?
- What support-staff permissions or interface should be used for manual unlocking?
